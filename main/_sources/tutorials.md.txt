# Tutorials

Set up your workstation, inspect a running application, then edit and debug its
source. The worked examples use a PMAC IOC and BlueAPI on P47; substitute your own
beamline or cluster details. DLS setup is shown first, with short alternatives
for other environments.

Follow setup once, then choose the workflow you need. Commands run on your
workstation unless a step says **in the seat**.
